Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TL;DR: Numinous only stores data necessary for functionality. We do not sell or share your data. You can request deletion at any time.

1. Information We Collect

Numinous collects and stores the following data to provide its core functionality:

  • User IDs: Used for saved playlists and track preferences.
  • Server IDs: Used to store server-specific music configurations.
  • Saved Tracks: Stored locally when users save songs to their playlist.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the bot's music features
  • To save and manage user playlists
  • To improve and optimize performance

3. Data Storage

  • Data is stored locally in a saved_playlists.json file
  • No external databases or third-party storage services are used
  • Data is not shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party

4. Data Retention

  • Data is retained indefinitely unless manually deleted or requested for removal
  • You can request deletion of your data by contacting the developer

5. Discord Developer Terms Compliance

Numinous complies with Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Platform Policy.
