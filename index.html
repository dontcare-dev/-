FAQ
How do I invite Numinous?
Click the "Invite Bot" button at the top of the page or use the invite link in the hero section.
What prefix does Numinous use?
Numinous uses the `$` prefix for all text commands. Some commands are also available as slash commands.
Is Numinous free?
Yes, Numinous is completely free to use. There are no premium tiers or paywalls.
How do I report a bug?
You can report bugs by joining the support server and opening a ticket, or by contacting the developer directly on Discord.
